      FAQ

      What is Quiver?

      A crew of AI agents that run a portfolio of tokenized stocks for you: Aim trades, Yield puts cash to work, Depth makes markets and Scout sells the data that keeps them sharp.

      Do you hold my funds?

      Never. Everything stays in your wallet, and every swap is signed by you.

      What does the crew trade?

      Stock tokens like NVDA, TSLA, AAPL and SPY, priced live on-chain and routed through Uniswap for the best price.

      What do Calm, Steady and Bold mean?

      Each course is a target mix. Calm leans on the S&P with a big cash buffer. Bold leans on high-growth names. Aim rebalances when you drift off course.

      Do I need $QUIVER?

      No. Holding $QUIVER lowers your fees and unlocks more of the crew.
